### How to Install the Extension

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Type chrome://extensions/ in the address bar.
3. In the top right corner, enable `Developer mode.`
4. Click the `Load unpacked` button in the top left corner.
5. Select the `aria2dm-extension` folder.
6. Your extension will appear!
7. Click details/options on the extension to open the Settings page, then enter your RPC IP and Token.
8. Right-click a download link/button and select `Download with Aria2DM.`

If successful, a notification from Chrome will appear in the corner of your screen. Click the icon to see the download list!
